# Risks & Compliance — read before selling the offer

These are the things that can get a client penalized (and your reputation with
them). Knowing them is also a selling point vs. cheaper competitors.

## Google Business Profile ("5 different Google Maps")
- Creating GBP listings for addresses a business does **not** physically staff
  violates Google's guidelines. These "lead-gen" listings get suspended, and
  reinstating a suspended legit listing is painful.
- Legit ways to get more map coverage:
  - Real additional locations (offices/branches) only.
  - Service-area business settings + strong service×location *pages* (on-site),
    which is where the programmatic SEO actually moves the needle.
  - Review velocity, GBP Posts, categories, photos on the real listing.

## Backlinks
- "High-quality backlinks" at scale is often PBNs or paid link networks →
  manual-action risk. We don't do that.
- What we *can* automate safely: local citations (NAP-consistent directory
  listings), niche/industry directories, internal linking, and *drafting*
  outreach for genuine editorial/guest placements (a human sends them).

## Programmatic pages
- Bulk service×location pages must be genuinely useful and differentiated, not
  thin spun duplicates, or they get filtered/ignored by Google. Our generation
  step writes unique local content + schema per page, not template mad-libs.

## Content
- Disclose AI use per client preference; keep facts (licenses, guarantees,
  pricing) human-verified before publish.
